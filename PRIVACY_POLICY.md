# Privacy Policy for Reference Helper

**Last Updated: 2025-12-05**

## 1. Introduction
Reference Helper ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information solely for the purpose of providing the extension's functionality:

*   **Web History & Website Content**: We collect the URLs, titles, and selected text of the web pages you visit or interact with. This is done only when you explicitly trigger the "Add to Reference List" feature or when the "Visit History" / "Copy Tracking" features are enabled by you.
*   **User Activity**: We can detect copy events (Ctrl+C) on web pages to provide the "Copy History" feature. **This feature is disabled by default.**

## 3. How We Use Your Information
The collected information is used exclusively for:
*   Creating and managing your reference lists.
*   Providing a history of visited pages and copied texts for your convenience.
*   Automatically inserting references into blog editors.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
*   **Local Storage**: All data is stored locally on your device within the Chrome browser's storage system.
*   **Sync Storage**: If you are signed in to Chrome, your data may be synced across your devices using Chrome's Sync API. This data is handled by Google's secure infrastructure.
*   **No External Servers**: **We do not transmit, store, or share your data on any external servers owned by us or third parties.** Your data remains entirely under your control.

> **⚠️ Important Security Notice**: The Copy History feature tracks text you copy from web pages. While we automatically filter common sensitive patterns (API keys, tokens, passwords, credit cards), you should be aware:
> - Some sensitive information may not be detected by our filters
> - If you regularly handle sensitive data, we recommend keeping "Copy Tracking" disabled (default)
> - You can clear your copy history at any time from the Options page
> - Sensitive data filtering can be disabled in settings if needed

## 5. Sharing of Information
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

## 6. Your Control Over Data
You can:
*   Enable or disable copy tracking in the extension's Options page
*   Clear all copy history at any time
*   Configure sensitive data filtering preferences
*   Export or delete all extension data

## 7. Your Consent
By using our extension, you consent to this privacy policy.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us via the support section on the Chrome Web Store.
